04Private by default

A connection you can see and approve.

Vamp borrows the good part of being local: the host is yours, the route is yours, and every new peer has to be visible and approved on the machine itself.

✓Terminal access stays opt-in. Display and input capabilities are separate from the shell.
01

Find the host

Your client discovers a signed host over Bonjour on your LAN or through your private Tailscale network.

02

Check the fingerprint

The Mac shows a pairing request and a code. Nothing connects while the request is still waiting for you.

03

Approve the session

Approve the exact device on the host, then connect. Revoke a peer later whenever you want.

05The short version

The useful kind of quiet.

Vamp gives you a capable remote surface without turning your workflow into an account, a dashboard, or a hosted dependency.

↗

Your network first

Connections use your LAN or Tailscale network. There is no project-owned relay waiting in the middle.

✓

Approved by hand

A signed peer identity and visible host approval are required before the session starts.

⌘

Ready for agents

The bundled CLI exposes status, pending requests, fingerprint checks, and terminal sessions for automation.
